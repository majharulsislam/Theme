Do you work with medical aid / insurance?
Yes — I work with most major South African medical aids and can provide documentation for claims. I also offer private-pay options. During your free consultation call we'll confirm your specific cover and discuss a fee structure that works for your situation.
What does an initial OT assessment involve?
The initial assessment typically takes 60–90 minutes and covers your occupational history, functional abilities, mental and physical health status, and your personal goals. From this I develop a tailored treatment plan with clear, measurable outcomes — not open-ended sessions.
How long does vocational rehabilitation take?
It depends on the complexity of your case, your diagnosis, and your employer's requirements. Typical programs run 4–12 weeks. I provide milestone-based progress reports throughout so all parties — including insurers and employers — are kept informed and on track.
Do you offer virtual / telehealth sessions?
Yes. Many components of OT consultation, vocational coaching, case management, and healthcare consulting can be delivered effectively via video session. This is especially useful for clients outside Johannesburg or those with limited mobility.
Can you provide medico-legal reports or capacity evaluations?
Absolutely. I have extensive experience in functional capacity evaluations and medico-legal documentation for attorneys, insurers, and employers. Please contact me directly to discuss your specific reporting requirements and timelines.
