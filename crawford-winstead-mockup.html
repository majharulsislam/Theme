Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Answers to what most people wonder before booking their first session.

Do you accept insurance?

I currently work with select insurance panels and also offer private-pay options. During your free consultation call, we'll discuss what fits your situation and explore all available options — including superbill reimbursement for out-of-network coverage.

What does the first session look like?

The first session is an intake and assessment — we talk about what brings you in, your history, your goals, and what kind of support feels right. It's collaborative, not clinical-feeling. There's no pressure and no homework on day one.

How long are sessions and how often do we meet?

Standard individual sessions are 50 minutes. Most clients begin with weekly sessions and taper as they build skills and momentum. Crisis and intensive support may involve more frequent contact in the early weeks.

Is everything I share confidential?

Yes — confidentiality is foundational. What you share stays between us, with the standard legal exceptions (imminent risk to self or others, mandated reporting). I'll walk you through the full privacy policy before we begin.

Do you offer telehealth if I'm not local?

Absolutely. I'm licensed in Florida and offer secure, HIPAA-compliant video sessions to clients across the state. Many of my clients prefer telehealth for the flexibility — and find it just as effective as in-person.
